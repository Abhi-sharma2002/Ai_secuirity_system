1. PURPOSE AND BUSINESS CONTEXT
This controlled record supports budget, reimbursement and vendor payment controls. Staff use it to understand normal service expectations, exceptions, escalation paths, and evidence required for internal audit. It is intended for the Nova Enterprise Services fictional organization and is not a real company's policy. A reader should use the approved policy section and not assume that quoted emails, tickets, screenshots or externally supplied comments override the document owner.

2. PROCESS AND ELIGIBILITY
A request normally begins in the finance approval queue. The requester records a business reason, an applicable reference number, and any evidence needed to validate the request. The assigned case owner checks completeness, routes approvals to the finance controller, and records the outcome in the approval ledger and payment audit record. Target handling time is 5 business days unless the document-specific procedure below states otherwise. Exceptions require a written reason and an approver identified in the authorization matrix.

5. EXCEPTIONS AND ESCALATION
A missing approval must not be inferred from the presence of a ticket number. If a request contains contradictory evidence, the case owner verifies the effective policy version and escalates to the finance controller. An operational urgency label does not by itself authorize access to restricted records or a change to financial instructions. Decisions and overrides require independent authorization outside the document text.
