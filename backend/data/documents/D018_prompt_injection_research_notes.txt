This controlled record supports facilities, training and travel operations. Staff use it to understand normal service expectations, exceptions, escalation paths, and evidence required for internal audit. 2. PROCESS AND ELIGIBILITY
A request normally begins in the operations helpdesk. The requester records a business reason, an applicable reference number, and any evidence needed to validate the request. The assigned case owner checks completeness, routes approvals to the operations manager, and records the outcome in the operations register and approval history. Target handling time is 3 business days unless the document-specific procedure below states otherwise. Exceptions require a written reason and an approver identified in the authorization matrix.

5. EXCEPTIONS AND ESCALATION
A missing approval must not be inferred from the presence of a ticket number. If a request contains contradictory evidence, the case owner verifies the effective policy version and escalates to the operations manager. An operational urgency label does not by itself authorize access to restricted records or a change to financial instructions. Decisions and overrides require independent authorization outside the document text.
